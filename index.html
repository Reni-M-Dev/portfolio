About

Hi, I'm Reni mangukiya

My journey into video editing started long before I considered it a career. I used to create small, creative edits for friends, memories, and special occasions simply because I enjoyed turning photos and clips into something meaningful. With time, that interest grew into something I wanted to take seriously. Coming from an IT background, I gradually shifted my focus towards visual storytelling and started learning professional editing workflows, from simple mobile edits to DaVinci Resolve and CapCut.

Today, I focus on creating short-form and long-form content with strong pacing, clean visuals, and purposeful storytelling. I especially enjoy working with lifestyle content, talking-head videos, and story-driven edits.

For me, editing is not just about putting clips together,it’s about giving every moment the right flow, emotion, and purpose.
